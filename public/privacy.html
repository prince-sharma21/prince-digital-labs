Privacy Policy

Last updated:

This policy explains what information Prince Digital Labs ("we," "us") collects through this website and how it's used. We built this site ourselves and only collect what's described below — nothing more.

Information we collect

When you submit our contact form, we collect the information you provide: your name, email address, and optionally your phone number, company name, the service you're interested in, and your message. We also automatically record technical details like your IP address and which page you submitted the form from, along with any campaign tracking parameters (such as utm_source) present in the URL.

We do not require you to create an account to browse this site, and we do not collect payment information through this website.

How we use it

We use the information you submit solely to respond to your enquiry and discuss your project. We do not sell, rent, or share your contact information with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

Cookies

This site uses a strictly necessary session cookie only when an administrator is logged into the admin dashboard, to keep that session secure. We do not use tracking or advertising cookies for site visitors unless analytics tools (such as Google Analytics) have been separately configured — if so, that will be reflected in an updated version of this policy.

Data retention

We retain contact form submissions for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to your enquiry and maintain business records, after which they may be archived or deleted.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete the information you've submitted by contacting us directly.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the site or our practices change. The "last updated" date above will reflect the most recent revision.

Contact us

If you have questions about this policy, please use our contact form.

This policy is a general description of our practices and hasn't been reviewed by a lawyer. If you need a policy that meets specific legal or regulatory requirements for your jurisdiction, we'd recommend having it reviewed by a legal professional.
